## Read Before Starting

- Cross-cutting rules are in `docs/agent-rules/` (security, testing, dependencies, observability). Read the ones relevant to the change.
- Step-by-step workflows are in `docs/agent-tasks/` (feature, bugfix, refactor, code-review, dependency-upgrade, db-migration). Read the matching one first.
- Human-facing conventions in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `README.md` still apply; they are not repeated here.
